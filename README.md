# 💧 AquaSignal

AquaSignal is an explainable AI platform that predicts water safety risk 
using real-world physicochemical parameters. Unlike basic safe/unsafe 
classifiers, AquaSignal tells you *why* water is dangerous, *which* 
parameters are critical, and *how* water quality is trending over time 
in your region.

Built on 20M+ global measurements from the UN GEMS/Water database and 
trained with explainable ML (SHAP), AquaSignal is designed for anyone 
on Earth — from rural communities to environmental researchers.

## 🔬 What it does
- Predicts water potability from physicochemical inputs
- Explains which parameters (pH, turbidity, sulfates etc.) are driving risk
- Shows regional water quality trends over time
- Runs as a free, open web app — no installation needed

## 🛠️ Built with
- Python, Pandas, Scikit-learn, XGBoost, SHAP
- Google Colab (development)
- Streamlit (web app)
- UN GEMS/Water + Kaggle datasets
